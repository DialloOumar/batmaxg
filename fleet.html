About Batmex

Equipment, haulage and field support for mining in Guinea.

BATMEX GROUP SARL provides heavy equipment rental, earthmoving, bulk haulage and operational support to mining and construction projects. Our machines and tipper trucks cover the full earthmoving cycle — digging, pushing, loading, hauling, grading and compaction.

Today, 48 of our machines and 45 trucks work under mining contracts, including for an internationally recognised operation: 45 trucks in the Boké region hauling 8,000 tonnes a day over 118 km, 9 machines in Kouroussa and 39 machines at Léro.
